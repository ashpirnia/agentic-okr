# ADR-NNNN — Short title in the imperative or as a statement

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Deciders:** Ash, Claude

## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? Include the constraint or pressure that makes it non-obvious. If there is a fact we learned the hard way, put it here rather than in a commit message.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense. Short enough to quote.

## Consequences

What becomes true as a result — good and bad. Include the costs we are accepting, not just the benefits. If this decision constrains a future one, say which.

## Alternatives rejected

Each alternative we seriously considered, and the specific reason it lost. This section is not optional. It is what stops us relitigating a settled decision in three weeks having forgotten the reasoning, and it is where the article material lives.
